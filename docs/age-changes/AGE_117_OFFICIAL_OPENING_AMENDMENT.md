# Age 117 Official Opening Amendments

**Source:** Official World of Legends Age 117 opening announcement  
**Announced:** 17 October 2026  
**Scheduled age start:** Tuesday, 20 October 2026 at 18:00 GMT

These are amendments to `UTOPIA_AGE_117_PREFINAL_CHANGES.pdf`. They take precedence over conflicting pre-final details.

## Gnome — Cunning Assault

Each successful attack grants one of these thievery boosts, each lasting 6 days:

- +25% offensive thievery effectiveness
- -25% thief losses
- +10% sabotage damage

The boosts are independent and can all be active at the same time. These are conditional Cunning Assault effects, not permanent passive race bonuses.

## Removed content

- Remove the **Ascendency** ritual.
- Remove the **Amethyst Dragon**.

## Verification note

The canonical Age 117 rules tables were checked after the announcement. Ascendency and Amethyst Dragon were already absent. The Gnome rule description already included the three Cunning Assault effects; the two effects that had also been listed as permanent bonuses (+10% Sabotage Damage and -25% Thief Losses) were removed from the permanent bonus list so they are represented as conditional ability effects instead.
